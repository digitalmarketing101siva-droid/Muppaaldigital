The idea behind the mark

Three pillars carry one growth strategy. A sprout rises from between them.

Midnight Blue pillar: Digital Foundation
Website, social and content. The base everything stands on.
Slate Blue pillar: Search Visibility
SEO, AEO and GEO. Being found by engines and AI platforms.
Copper pillar: Business Growth
Branding, demand generation and conversion. Visibility turned into results.
Two leaves: sprouting and growth
The slate leaf is what is taking root. The copper leaf is momentum. The leaves are separated from the pillars by a thin gap in the background colour.

The pillar colours match the three tiers of the Muppaal Framework, so the logo and the framework graphic always read as one system.

Clear space

Keep a margin around the logo equal to the width of the middle pillar, measured from every edge of the lockup. Nothing else enters this zone.

Minimum size

Full lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Icon only: 24 px or 8 mm. Below these sizes, switch to the icon alone.

Proportion

Roughly 50% Warm Neutral, 22% Midnight Blue, 10% Slate Blue, 10% Charcoal, 8% Copper. Copper stays rare so it keeps its signal.

Contrast

Set body text in Charcoal or Midnight Blue on Warm Neutral. Copper on Warm Neutral is too light for small text, so use it for large headings, icons, rules and button fills with Midnight Blue or white labels at 18 px and above.

Voice

Clear, confident and practical. We explain the strategy, then show the outcome.

We write like this

  • Plain verbs and specific outcomes: “Get found by AI search.”
  • Short sentences, sentence case.
  • Evidence before claims.

We avoid this

  • Hype words such as “revolutionary” or “guru”.
  • Unexplained jargon. Spell out AEO and GEO on first use.
  • Promises we cannot measure.

Logo misuse

Keep the mark as drawn.

Do

  • Use the supplied files at the sizes above.
  • Place on Warm Neutral, white or Midnight Blue.
  • Use the reversed version on dark photography with a Midnight Blue overlay.

Don’t

  • Recolour the pillars or swap their order.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Remove the leaves or change their colours.
  • Place the full-colour logo on Copper or busy images.
  • Use more than one logo on a single surface.
